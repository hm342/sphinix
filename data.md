    displays **9+ years of proven excellence**. Preserve the wording
    from the profile, but confirm the current year-based experience
    figure before publishing or updating the website.
-   The statistics **150+ happy clients**, **700+ projects delivered**,
    and **15+ industries served** are profile claims. Confirm whether
    these figures should remain unchanged on the live website.
-   The email-marketing ROI statement (**\$36 for every \$1 spent**) is
    a claim printed in the profile. Confirm its source and whether the
    company wants to publish it as a general claim.
-   The phrase **"No Ban Risk"** is a product claim shown for ChatBox
    360 Suite. Confirm the exact approved wording and any applicable
    platform-policy qualifications before publishing.
-   The client logo spellings should be checked against original logo
    assets, especially where the compressed PDF makes lettering
    difficult to read.
-   The testimonial screenshots should be replaced with original,
    high-resolution assets or verified review text. Do not invent
    reviewer names, ratings, or quotations.
-   The PDF shows technology and partner logos. Use only the logos and
    partnership/certification claims that the company is authorized to
    display.
-   The PDF includes QR codes for product demos/experiences. Use the
    original QR-code image assets and test each code before publishing.
-   Service workflows are explicitly described as potentially varying
    according to project scope, business goals, industry, platform
    policies and requirements.
-   The profile does not provide individual service prices, package
    names, guarantees, a contact form specification, social profile
    URLs, or complete client testimonial transcripts. These details
    should not be invented.
